# Contributing

Please submit a case with a stable original source, the complete prompt published by its creator, the model and variant evidence, all required reference inputs, the corresponding original output, and permission or an applicable license for both prompt and media. Do not infer a prompt from an image or label an editorial rewrite as the one that generated it. Credit the creator and identify any changes and license conditions. Avoid duplicate prompts and outputs.

Examples described as generated by a creator or third-party service should be labeled accordingly. Do not claim an unverified model variant. Maintainers review provenance, licenses and media before accepting a case.
